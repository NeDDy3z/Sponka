# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Sponka is an open source notes app for Android. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not run any servers that receive your data.

## Your notes stay on your device

Notes, canvases, PDFs, imported files, audio recordings and settings are stored on your device. We never see them.

## When the app connects to the internet

The app only talks to the services below, and only for the features you use.

- **Cloud sync (Filen or Nextcloud).** If you turn on sync, your files go to the Filen account or Nextcloud server you sign in to. Filen data is end-to-end encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. Your login details are kept encrypted with the Android Keystore and are only sent to that service. Their own privacy policies apply to the data they store.
- **Handwriting to LaTeX with your own LLM.** If you set up an LLM provider (for example OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter or a custom endpoint), the image of the handwriting you choose to convert is sent to that provider together with your API key. Nothing is sent unless you configure this and run a conversion. The provider's privacy policy applies.
- **Model downloads.** Handwriting recognition models are downloaded through Google ML Kit, and the maths recognition model is downloaded from GitHub. Recognition itself runs on your device. Google and GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when serving these files.
- **Update checks.** By default the app asks GitHub for the latest release when it starts. You can turn this off in Settings. GitHub may log your IP address.
- **Links.** Opening a link (for example to GitHub) opens it in your browser.

## Permissions

- **Microphone** - only to record audio notes when you start a recording. Recordings stay on your device unless you sync or share them.
- **Notifications and foreground services** - to show progress for recording, sync, downloads and exports.
- **Internet and network state** - for the features listed above.
- **Install packages** - to install an update you chose to download.
- **Ignore battery optimizations** - so long syncs are not stopped by the system, only if you allow it.

## Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your data. The only data that leaves your device goes to the services listed above, and only because you used that feature.

## Children

Sponka is not aimed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Data you synced to Filen or Nextcloud, or sent to an LLM provider, is managed by that service.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this file and at https://sponka.neddy.dev/privacy-policy.
